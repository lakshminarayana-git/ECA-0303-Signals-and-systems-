# ECA0303 – Signals and Systems Laboratory Experiments

## 📘 Course Information

- **Course Code:** ECA0303
- **Course Name:** Signals and Systems Laboratory

---

## 📖 About

This repository contains MATLAB implementations of the  laboratory experiment of the **ECA0303 – Signals and Systems** course.

The official laboratory manual consists of **10 major experiments**, each containing **4 sub-experiments**. This repository includes **only Experiments X.1 and X.2** for each major experiment. 

---

## 🧪 Experiments Included

### Experiment 1 – Signal Generation and Basic Signal Operations
- **Experiment 1.1:** Generation of Continuous-Time Signals
- **Experiment 1.2:** Generation of Discrete-Time Signals

### Experiment 2 – Signal Properties and Transformations
- **Experiment 2.1:** Periodic and Aperiodic Signals
- **Experiment 2.2:** Verification of Even and Odd Signal Decomposition

### Experiment 3 – Sequence Operations and Signal Analysis
- **Experiment 3.1:** Addition and Multiplication of Two Sequences
- **Experiment 3.2:** Energy and Power Calculation of Given Signals

### Experiment 4 – System Analysis and Classification
- **Experiment 4.1:** Discrete-Time System Analysis
- **Experiment 4.2:** Causal and Non-Causal System Analysis

### Experiment 5 – System Response and LTI Properties
- **Experiment 5.1:** Impulse Response of the System
- **Experiment 5.2:** Step Response of the System

### Experiment 6 – Convolution and Sampling Techniques
- **Experiment 6.1:** Convolution of Continuous-Time Signals
- **Experiment 6.2:** Convolution of Discrete-Time Signals

### Experiment 7 – Signal Reconstruction and Spectral Analysis
- **Experiment 7.1:** Reconstruction of Signal from Samples (Ideal Interpolation)
- **Experiment 7.2:** Fourier Series of Half-Wave Rectifier Output

### Experiment 8 – Fourier Transform and Frequency Domain Analysis
- **Experiment 8.1:** Fourier Transform of Periodic Impulse Train
- **Experiment 8.2:** Discrete-Time Fourier Transform (DTFT)

### Experiment 9 – DFT and FFT Algorithms
- **Experiment 9.1:** Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT)
- **Experiment 9.2:** Circular Convolution

### Experiment 10 – Transform Techniques and System Representation
- **Experiment 10.1:** Pole-Zero Plot
- **Experiment 10.2:** Z-Transform and Inverse Z-Transform

## ▶️ Running the Programs

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open MATLAB.
3. Navigate to the required experiment folder.
4. Open the `.m` file.
5. Click **Run** or press **F5**.
